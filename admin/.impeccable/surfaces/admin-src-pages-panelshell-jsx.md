## Scope

Phone composition of both Epsilon panels (staff `PanelShell`, group `GroupShell`) plus the shared systems they need on phone and PC: pill dock, page heads, ambient accent backdrop with light glyph rain, detail sheet (bottom sheet on phone, centred dialog on PC). Visitor mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

Staff and group admins on Telegram phones (often weak Android) checking live numbers and acting on players, cases and rights with one thumb.

## Constraints

Keep every function, permission gate, data source and Russian copy meaning. No invented numbers. Effects must switch off under reduced motion and degrade under the optimization toggle. World is the existing Epsilon Craft system (DESIGN.md); the owner's six references pin the composition: bento tiles, pill dock, tile → sheet.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone panel is a pocket instrument — each tab is a short stack of graphite tiles whose numbers are touchable and unfold into a sheet with the whole story. It refuses the desktop admin squeezed onto a phone: nested cards, kickers over headings, duplicated titles, dense forms first.

OWN-WORLD: True black ground lit from above by a soft aurora of the user's palette color; graphite tiles #1c1c1e / #2c2c2e at 26px corners; white Manrope 750 tabular numerals; one accent-filled capsule marks the active dock tab; round 44px icon buttons; sparse accent glyph rain drifting only in the top band, 15–20% opacity.

STORY: The user opens a tab, reads the live figure at once, taps any tile to see its trend and comparison, and takes the next action from the sheet without hunting through the page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone — 32px title left with its one-line context, round tool buttons right; a wide accent-lit hero tile with the tab's main live number and period chips; two square tiles below it with secondary numbers and ↗ open marks; floating pill dock 12px above the bottom edge, active tab expanded with icon and label in accent, the menu as a separate circle to its right. PC — same tiles in a wider bento, the sheet opens centred.

FORM: Bento stack plus tile-to-sheet unfold. Pinned by the owner's references (position 1 of 1); no concept-seed roll, brief-pinned direction beats the roll. Seed key: none (brief-pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

Tapping a metric tile: the tile lifts and unfolds into a sheet (bottom on phone, centre on PC) showing the number, the change against the previous period and a bar chart of all periods, with the next action as a button.
